Privacy Policy

FRC 6998 Scouting PASS — Last updated: February 2026

1. About This App

FRC 6998 Scouting PASS is a free, open-source match scouting tool developed by FRC Team 6998 / NNKIEH (National Nanke International Experimental High School) for use during FIRST Robotics Competition events.

2. Data Collection

We do not collect any personal data. The app does not require an account, does not track users, and does not use any analytics or advertising services.

3. Local Storage

All scouting data (match records, settings, offline queue) is stored exclusively in your device's localStorage. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to export or sync it.

4. Optional Google Sheets Sync

The app offers an optional feature to upload scouting data to a Google Sheets spreadsheet via Google Apps Script. This action is:

  • Entirely user-initiated (you must tap "Save & Submit")
  • Transmits only match scouting data (team numbers, scores, robot observations)
  • Does not transmit any personal or device information

5. No Third-Party Services

The app works fully offline. It does not load any external CDNs, analytics scripts, or third-party tracking services. All assets (fonts, styles, scripts) are bundled within the app.

6. Children's Privacy

This app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. It is designed for use by FRC team members at competition events.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
